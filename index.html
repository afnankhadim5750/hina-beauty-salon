Our Story

A Rosemont salon built on one gentle craft

Hina beauty salon, at 2033 Rosemont Blvd in Montréal, is known first for threading — the gentle cotton-thread technique, with no chemicals and no heat, for crisp lines and a finish that lasts. Brows are shaped to your face, and every detail, from forehead to chin, is finished with the same care.

Around that signature, the salon offers thorough, careful waxing with skin-soothing aftercare, and makeup from an evening glow to full bridal — long-wear, camera-ready looks matched to your skin tone and your event.

Everything is performed in-salon by our specialists. Tell us about sensitive skin when you book and we'll adapt — your comfort comes first.

In the Treatment Room

Your appointment, moment by moment

No rush, no guesswork — this is how a visit at Hina unfolds, from the chair to the finish.

02

The treatment itself

Threading with the gentle cotton-thread technique — no chemicals, no heat — for crisp lines and a finish that lasts. Waxing is thorough and careful, and makeup is matched to your skin tone and your event.
